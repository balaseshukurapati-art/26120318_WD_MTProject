The Story: Why Hardware, Economics & Film?

FOUNDER ORIGIN STORY

From Sketchbook Blueprints to Seed Funding

Growing up between coastal Srikakulam and the growing university hub of Amaravati, I noticed a stark contrast: rapid digital ambition alongside persistent gaps in basic rural and disaster-relief sanitation. Conventional mobile toilets were too heavy to transport, relied on polluting chemical tanks, and cost too much for local panchayats or relief camps to scale.

“An engineering prototype solves the physical problem, economics makes it affordable at scale, and storytelling convinces people to adopt it.”

That realization shaped my path at SRM University-AP. While pursuing my BBA in Digital Business & Entrepreneurship and a parallel degree in Economics, I sketched a collapsible, source-separating washroom from scratch—and pitched it at the SRM-AP Sociothon, winning 1st place and seed funding to bring the model to life.

THE INTERDISCIPLINARY EDGE

Three Lenses on Every Problem

1. The Builder (Hardware & Digital): Whether assembling Schedule-40 UPVC scissor-hinge frames or coding responsive web platforms, I believe in rapid, low-cost prototyping over endless theory.

2. The Economist (Unit Economics): Studying microeconomics and public policy taught me how to turn sanitation waste into circular fertilizer revenue and structure B2G (Business-to-Government) subsidies.

3. The Filmmaker (Human Empathy): Winning the collegiate Short Film Festival sharpened my ability to observe human behavior, frame compelling narratives, and pitch high-stakes ideas with clarity.

Flagship Venture: EEDS Modular Portable Washroom

★ SRM UNIVERSITY-AP SOCIOTHON WINNER & SEED-FUNDED PROTOTYPE

Zero-Sewage, Collapsible Sanitation System (100 × 100 × 190 cm)

Engineered for rapid deployment in rural communities, flood/disaster relief zones, and high-footfall public gatherings. Unlike single-pit latrines that contaminate groundwater, our EEDS (Ecological Source-Diverting) architecture separates urine, solid waste, and wash-water at the point of use—eliminating odor and enabling safe nutrient recovery.

PHASE 01 // STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING

Collapsible UPVC & 250 GSM Cabin

Built with Schedule-40 UPVC scissor-hinge X-poles, steel pivot joints, and a waterproof 250 GSM royal-blue tarpaulin jacket with a 6-inch mosquito-net ventilation skirt. Packs flat for low-cost rural transport and anchors via steel ground J-pegs.

PHASE 02 // 3-WAY SOURCE SEPARATION

Zero-Contamination Waste Plan

Hole 1 (Front): Sloped urine drain routed to a sealed 10L jerrycan for nitrogen-rich liquid fertilizer. Hole 2 (Center): Solid waste drop into a 20L airtight green HDPE drum with bio-bag & ash scoop. Hole 3 (Rear): Wash-water routed to a covered gravel soak-pit.

PHASE 03 // GO-TO-MARKET & ECONOMICS

Seed-Funded Pilot & B2G Model

Using our SRM-AP Sociothon seed grant to fabricate field-ready units equipped with a 5L gravity handwash pouch and solar LED lantern. Targeting district disaster-response authorities, rural NGOs, and event organizers under a low-capex lease/sale model.
